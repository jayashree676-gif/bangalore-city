Design notes

Where does Bengaluru end? In 3D.

The same installation, a 5.8 m wall with a 55" screen on rails, rebuilt as a miniature 3D world. Every stop shows only its year and one object. The frame is still the lens: outside it the world is a grey archive, inside it everything comes back in colour.

How it works

  • One object per year. At rest, each stop shows a single hero piece, floating and turning gently, to invite you over.
  • The frame builds the memory. As it slides over a stop, the rest of the scene flies in piece by piece, tied to how far you have slid. Slide away and it leaves again.
  • Restore it, earn it. When a memory is complete, you earn that stop’s stamp. 7 stamps unlock 2037, where you build the city you want.
  • Click to move. Click another year’s object to glide there. Click the current scene to read its full story.

Built with

  • Three.js for the 3D world, with a two-pass render for the grey/colour lens.
  • StringTune for smooth scrolling, split-text reveals and magnetic buttons.
