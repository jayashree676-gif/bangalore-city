# Where does Bengaluru end? · Digital Slider

A web simulation of a museum installation designed for the DVSI UI/UX assignment: a 5800 × 1400 mm backlit printed wall with a 55" portrait touch screen (1080 × 1920) that slides along it on rails.

The story is told by one of Kempegowda's watchtowers, built to mark where the city would end. The city broke past it, again and again.

**Everything is in one file: `index.html`.** No build step. Open it in a modern desktop browser.

## The experience

- **The screen is a lens.** The printed wall is a grey archive. Wherever the screen stops, it shows the same wall in colour.
- **8 stops, 1537 to 2037.** Each stop from 1537 to 2007 has one hands-on moment. Do it to earn that stop's stamp.
- **Collect 7 stamps, unlock the future.** Each stamp unlocks a building block. With all 7, 2037 opens and you build the Bengaluru you want, then share it as a link.
- **The story breaks out of the frame.** At a stop, the year, title and narration reveal around the screen. Scroll down and the screen opens into a full chapter page.

## Controls

- Drag the screen's side handles, or use ← → keys
- Scroll down at a stop to open the full story; scroll up past the top to close it
- `V` switches between wall view and follow view, `H` shows how the wall is measured
- Design notes has a reviewer shortcut that grants all 7 stamps

## Links

- `#stop-1905` opens straight at a stop
- `#city-…` opens a city someone built
