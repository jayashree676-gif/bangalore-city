Design notes

Where does Bengaluru end?

A 5.8 m printed timeline and a 55" touch screen on rails, told by one of Kempegowda’s watchtowers. The towers were built to mark the city’s edge. The city broke past them, again and again.

The idea

  • A lens, not a second screen. The print is a grey archive. The screen shows the same wall in colour.
  • Collect 7 stamps. Each stop has one hands-on moment. Each stamp unlocks a block for 2037.
  • Build the future. 2037 is blank on the wall. With all 7 stamps, visitors build the city they want.

On the web: beyond the frame

  • The slider stays true to the real wall. The story breaks out around it, at a size people can actually read.
  • Scroll down at a stop and the screen opens into a full chapter.
  • The 2037 builder takes over the whole browser: the reward for the journey.

Interaction rules

  • Position is navigation. No menus on the real screen.
  • Every stop ends with a hook to the next one.
  • Back and timeout always work.
