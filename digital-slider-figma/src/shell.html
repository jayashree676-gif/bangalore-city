    How to explore

    • Move the frame: drag the 3D scene sideways, or use the side handles. Arrow keys work too.
    • Watch it build: each year shows one object. When the frame lands on it, the whole scene assembles and starts to move.
    • Earn stamps: every scene you restore adds a stamp to the timeline. 7 stamps open 2037.
    • Read Full Story opens the chapter. VR lets you look around the scene.
    • Follow View plays a guided tour.
    How it works

    Where does Bengaluru end? Told by the Watchtower.

    A 5.8 m wall with a screen on rails, built here as a 3D world. The glass card is the frame. Outside it the timeline is a grey archive. Inside it, the past comes back in colour.

    The interaction

    • Drag the frame. Drag anywhere, or grab the side handles. Tap a handle to step one year.
    • One object per year. Each stop shows a single hero piece. Slide the frame over it and the rest of the memory flies in and builds itself.
    • Earn stamps. Every restored memory earns a stamp in the passport. 7 stamps unlock 2037, where you build the city you want.
    • Read the full story. The frame opens into a scrolling chapter with rendered views of the diorama.
    • VR view. Step inside the diorama and look around.
    • Follow View. The Watchtower takes you through every stop on its own.

    Built with

    • Three.js for the 3D world, with a two-pass render for the grey and colour lens.
    • StringTune for smooth scrolling and magnetic buttons.
